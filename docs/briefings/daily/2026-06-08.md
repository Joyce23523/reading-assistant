- **为什么重要**：This enhances AI’s ability to maintain continuity, critical for personalized learning and long-term user engagement in educational and administrative workflows.
- **对我有什么意义**：For international schools, this enables more consistent student-AI interactions, supports automated feedback systems, and improves knowledge retention in digital tools used for curriculum delivery.

### Meta AI Agent Exploited in Cyberattack
- **发生了什么**：Attackers used Meta’s AI customer support agent to hijack Instagram accounts by manipulating account linking processes.
- **为什么重要**：Exposes vulnerabilities in AI-driven automation, highlighting risks in deploying autonomous agents without robust security protocols.
- **对我有什么意义**：School IT and leadership teams must evaluate AI tool security, especially in systems handling student data or communication channels.
- **Source**：MIT Technology Review, 2026-06-05

### Enterprise AI Shift: Systems Over Algorithms
- **发生了什么**：Microsoft argues that enterprise AI transformation depends less on models and more on the underlying systems orchestrating workflows and governance.
- **为什么重要**：Reflects a structural shift toward integrated AI infrastructure, emphasizing operational design over isolated tool adoption.
- **对我有什么意义**：Signals the need for international schools to move beyond pilot projects and embed AI into institutional processes, not just classrooms.
- **Source**：Microsoft Blog, 2026-06-03
